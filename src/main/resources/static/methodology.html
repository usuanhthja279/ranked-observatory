HOW TO READ THIS OBSERVATORY

Methodology

This site reports saved leaderboard evidence and clearly labels where it uses estimates or incomplete historical observations.

Source rows and counts

  • A displayed player count is the number of saved source rows in the selected leaderboard scope. A row is not a verified unique person or account population.
  • Current and archived Acts use the selected source snapshot. Archived Acts are point-in-time records; the archive does not imply a continuous within-Act history.
  • Regional and global sources can have different coverage. The UI names which ladder or partition supports a comparison and does not silently combine incomplete sources.

Ranks, tiers and change

  • Rank order follows the selected leaderboard source. RR and rank movement are comparable only within their displayed region and observation window.
  • Tier labels remain source-reported when available. Combined Immortal labels are not silently split into subtiers. Any reconstructed or estimated tier value is labeled separately.
  • Change summaries use saved observations within a bounded window. Carried-forward, ambiguous, insufficient or unavailable observations are excluded or called out. A change is not an explanation of its cause.

Country labels and estimates

  • Country is a source label, not proof of nationality or current location. Unknown values remain visible.
  • Country allocation models are policy-based estimates, not observed facts. Their methods and limitations are described beside the relevant controls.

Freshness and availability

The last completed snapshot time is shown on the relevant page. When collection is paused or a source is incomplete, the UI reports that state rather than implying live coverage.
