Measured network data and exclusions

Publisher methodology · Published aggregates · Riot server locations

Actual browser measurements reach nearby cloud endpoints. They do not measure Riot’s UDP route, matchmaking queue quality, or a national population sample. Country tests are not unique players. Likely VPN-exit countries, insufficient samples, missing mapped cells and data older than 30 days get factor 1. No guessed pings fill gaps.

Quality = exp(−median RTT / 60 ms) × HTTPS reach rate. Best three qualities have weights 70/20/10. Relative access is compared with a population-weighted reference among eligible measured countries in the same shard. The ±10% raw weight change is multiplied by 0.25 for cloud proxies, matched-server coverage, sample sufficiency and the checked-test fraction. Thus the maximum proxy adjustment is ±2.5%, usually smaller. These are explicit policy parameters, not fitted causal effects on rank.

Policy formula and comparison controls

Country strength = 3 × Radiants + 2 × Immortal 3 + all Immortal 2. Upper-group weight = country count in that group × country strength. Lower-group weight = country population × (1 + clamp(country median RR, 0, 150) / 150). Unknown counts are allocated by each tier’s normalized weights; all Immortal 2 uses the upper rule with no RR split. Official source tiers are unchanged.

Previous tier model retains the former country-tier allocation with the same reported recoveries as the new policy. Original α model is the original AP/BR α=250 smoothing or pooled fallback before recoveries. Previous RR split + access retains the former 150 RR boundary; All Immortal 2 + access merges both portions and includes all Immortal 2 in country strength. Source inventory, recoveries and access factors are identical; their difference isolates removal of the split. Median adjustment is capped at 2× and falls back to population-only when unavailable. The policy does not claim independently measured prediction accuracy.

Reported countries are recovered only from recent consistent source observations or cached identity-checked profiles; conflicting reports remain Unknown. Each shard, tier and allocation band conserves its totals. Unknown groups without support remain unallocated.
