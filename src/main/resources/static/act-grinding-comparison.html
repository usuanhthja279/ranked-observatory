Formula and assumptions

Reported countries take priority, followed by consistent recent reported-country recovery. Unknown accounts are matched within the same shard and current tier to known accounts with comparable act-win totals and retained rank trajectories. More known accounts in a matching group give a country a larger share. No country receives a manual penalty or server-distance bonus.

Fixed act-win bands: 0–9, 10–19, 20–29, 30–39, 40–59 and 60+. Rank-history groups: previously higher tier; promoted; 25+ RR below observed peak; 25+ RR above first observation; otherwise within 25 RR; or insufficient history. History groups require three distinct observations spanning 60 minutes.

P(country | tier, group) = (group country count + 10 × tier country share) / (group known total + 10)

Probabilities are multiplied by Unknown counts in each group, summed, then rounded to preserve regional tier totals. Unsupported cells fall back to tier shares. Carried or ambiguous current statistics do not supply activity features. Repeated timestamps are deduplicated and conflicting history is excluded. The read-only export uses all retained complete current-act snapshots, including legacy AP history when present, capped at the comparison snapshot.

Smoothing strength 10 was fixed before inspecting country estimates. Sensitivity runs use 5, 20 and 50. Missing-country odds remain equal because no independent reporting-coverage audit exists. Source country flags and production estimates are unchanged.
