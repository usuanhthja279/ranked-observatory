How activity totals are estimated

Estimated player-matches = recorded wins added + estimated losses. Each observed RR-decrease interval is assumed to be one loss. These are tracked player results, not unique match lobbies: a single game can contribute results for several players. Both observations must be in the selected window; carried, duplicate and conflicting data is excluded. Country estimates are not used, and Unknown country is excluded from the top ten. Country links open the most active region for that country in the selected period. Counts can miss unobserved or offsetting results.

How movement is counted

Active Players counts distinct players with an observed change in RR, tier or wins. Total Wins is the recorded win increase. Estimated Losses assumes one loss per observed RR-decrease interval. Players +RR / −RR uses net observed RR change; players ending at zero net RR appear in neither group.

Both source observations must fall inside the selected window. Duplicate, carried or conflicting statistics, country changes and win resets are excluded. Players can cross multiple tier boundaries in either direction. Coverage can miss matches or offsetting results between snapshots. Country estimates do not affect this section.

Metric definitions and coverage

Approximate country totals (≈), not observed identities. Each unknown rank-tier total is distributed among observed countries; estimated Players is the sum of estimated rank tiers. Observed columns and individual country labels stay unchanged. The Unknown row is the original source total and is not added again. Missing countries may follow a different distribution, so these are model-based allocations, not verified nationality or future forecasts.

How country estimates work

Reported countries take priority. Unknown accounts are first matched to consistent recent leaderboard reports or a cached, identity-checked profile country. Conflicting reports remain unknown. Source counts and flags are unchanged; estimated totals include reported recoveries before allocating the remainder.

Country strength S = 3 × Radiants + 2 × Immortal 3 + all Immortal 2
Upper weight = country count in that group × S
Lower weight = country population × (1 + clamp(country median RR, 0, 150) / 150)
Final weight = rank/population weight × network-access factor
Estimated count = observed + recovered + remaining Unknown in group × final weight / sum of weights

The upper rule applies to all Radiant, Immortal 3 and Immortal 2 accounts. The lower rule applies to Immortal 1. Immortal 2 has no RR split. Median RR uses unambiguous reported statistics after country recovery; unavailable medians use the population-only multiplier of 1.

The network-access factor uses published country-level browser HTTPS measurements to matching cloud locations, not direct Riot game ping. It is capped and reduced for proxy type, partial server coverage and data quality. Missing, insufficient, stale or likely-VPN data gets 1×. Measurement source and limitations.

Each shard is allocated independently. Countries with greater upper-rank counts and a stronger upper-rank mix receive a compounded upper share. The 3/2/1 coefficients and capped lower-rank median adjustment are explicit product policy, not a calibrated prediction of nationality or skill. Integer rounding preserves every regional tier and RR-group total; unsupported groups remain unallocated. The model does not change source observations or assign guessed country flags.
